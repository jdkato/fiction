Years earlier, Monica had packed his bag before he asked for it. The door came off its hinges.

"Get out," she said. He said, "Fine." "No," he said. "Run!" she said. "Sure," he said.

The door opened. A step on the stair. She ran, and her pulse was in her ears. She was exhausted.

Dead leaves covered the ground. He was dishonest. He was ambitious. It was different from before. It bore on the outcome. The roads were impassable. At last, however, they reached camp. He meant to inquire with care.

"Nothin' doin'," he said. "I'm not going anywhere." Go. Now. Run.
